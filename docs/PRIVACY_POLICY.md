## 1. About CourtCheck

CourtCheck is a mobile application for recreational pickleball players. It helps players view participating facilities, see aggregate court activity, report temporary facility conditions, and check in at a facility. The application also includes administrative tools for authorized CourtCheck administrators.

This Privacy Policy explains how [OPERATOR LEGAL NAME] ("CourtCheck," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and protects information when you use CourtCheck.

## 2. Information we collect

### Account and profile information

We collect or generate the information needed to create and maintain a CourtCheck account, including:

- Your mobile phone number and phone-verification status.
- An optional email address, if you provide one.
- Your confirmation that you are at least 18 years old and the time of that confirmation.
- A system-generated anonymous username.
- Your selected pickleball experience level.
- Your account role, such as player or administrator.

CourtCheck uses one-time verification codes sent by text message instead of passwords. We do not ask you to provide your SMS verification code anywhere other than the CourtCheck verification screen.

### Facility activity and check-ins

When you check in or interact with facility features, we may collect:

- The facility associated with a check-in.
- Check-in, scheduled-expiration, and checkout times.
- The reason a check-in ended.
- Temporary facility-status reports you submit, such as courts being closed or a tournament taking place.
- Facility changes made through administrator tools.

CourtCheck preserves check-in history to operate the service, provide aggregate facility activity, enforce check-in rules, and support safety, security, and troubleshooting. Public facility-activity views are designed not to expose internal authentication identifiers.

### Location information

CourtCheck requests foreground location access when you initiate a check-in. Your submitted coordinates are used to determine whether you are within the selected facility's geofence. The database performs the authoritative geofence check.

The current version does not use background location and is designed not to store the submitted check-in coordinates in the check-in record. [VERIFY THESE STATEMENTS AGAINST THE PRODUCTION RELEASE BEFORE PUBLICATION.]

### Technical and service information

We and service providers acting for us may process technical information necessary to deliver and secure CourtCheck, such as IP address, device and app information, authentication events, session information, request timestamps, delivery status, error information, and security or fraud signals. The precise information retained by a service provider is also governed by that provider's terms and privacy documentation.

## 3. How we use information

We use information to:

- Create, authenticate, and secure CourtCheck accounts.
- Send user-requested one-time authentication codes.
- Complete onboarding and maintain player profiles.
- Validate check-ins and enforce the one-active-check-in rule.
- Display aggregate facility activity without unnecessarily exposing user identifiers.
- Operate facility-status and administrator features.
- Prevent abuse, fraud, unauthorized access, and violations of our Terms of Service.
- Diagnose errors, maintain the service, and respond to support or privacy requests.
- Comply with applicable law and enforce our legal rights.

## 4. Authentication text messages and consent

CourtCheck sends authentication text messages only after a user enters a mobile number and affirmatively requests a verification code. These messages are transactional and are not marketing messages.

Message frequency varies based on authentication requests. One message is ordinarily sent for each initial request, with additional messages sent only when a user requests another code. Message and data rates may apply. Reply **HELP** for help or **STOP** to opt out. Opting out may prevent you from receiving verification codes and therefore may prevent sign-in while phone authentication is CourtCheck's only authentication method.

## 5. How we disclose information

We may disclose information in the following limited circumstances:

- **Service providers.** Providers process information on our behalf to operate CourtCheck. These may include Supabase for authentication and database services, Twilio for delivery of authentication text messages, and Expo or applicable app-distribution providers for mobile application services.
- **Legal and safety reasons.** We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law or legal process; protect users, the public, or CourtCheck; investigate fraud or abuse; or enforce our agreements.
- **Business changes.** Information may be transferred as part of a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, bankruptcy, or sale of assets, subject to applicable law and the restrictions below concerning messaging consent.
- **At your direction.** We may disclose information when you direct us to do so or otherwise provide valid consent.

We do not sell or rent personal information. We do not use authentication-message consent for third-party marketing.

**All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information won’t be shared with any third parties.** Providers acting solely on our behalf may process only the minimum information needed to deliver and support the text-messaging service and may not use that information for their own marketing.
